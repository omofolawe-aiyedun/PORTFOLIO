About

Micheal builds websites for local businesses.

I am a web developer. I help a shop, studio, or service put a clear website online, then keep that site easy for customers to use.

How a project works

  1. 1. Listen

    We talk about the business, the customers, and what the website must do.

  2. 2. Plan and design

    We choose the pages and the order of the information before any visual styling.

  3. 3. Build

    I build the site, then we look at it on a computer and on a phone before it goes live.

  4. 4. Test and refine

    I check the layout, links, and key actions so the experience stays clear across screen sizes.

  5. 5. Deliver and support

    After launch, I can help keep the content current with practical updates and fixes.

Skills

Development

This is the stronger side of the work. I build with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and I can add PHP and MySQL when a site needs to store or send information.

Design

The design stays practical: a clear heading, readable text, steady spacing, one accent color, and a layout that works on a phone. The goal is a site customers can use, not a trend for its own sake.
