Welcome

Come hungry. Leave talking about the jollof.

Hearth & Palm is a Nigerian restaurant built around familiar flavours: party jollof, asun, ofada with ayamase, and a pot of soup with your choice of swallow.

Whether you are stopping in during the week or gathering the family on Sunday, the table is set for home-style cooking with restaurant care.
