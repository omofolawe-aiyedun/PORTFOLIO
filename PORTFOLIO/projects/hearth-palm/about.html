About Hearth & Palm

Taste of Naija, cooked with care.

Hearth & Palm serves authentic Nigerian food: the smoky party jollof that tastes like celebration, pepper soup that clears the head, ofada rice with ayamase, and swallow with soups that remind you of home.

The kitchen leans on palm oil, iru, fresh peppers, and slow-cooked meats. Small chops for sharing sit beside full plates of rice and soup. Drinks stay local too: zobo, palm wine, and a proper Chapman.

Come for lunch, come with family, or come because you have been craving asun. The room is named for two things Nigerian cooking depends on: fire, and the palm.
